- **H2 Step 1: Get your Google review link** (Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" / share review form; shorten it; use it everywhere). Mention the free QR generator.

  - Restaurants, cafés and pubs: when the bill comes (table coin at `/collections/medium-coins`)
  - Salons, barbers and beauty: at the mirror or the reception desk (stand)
  - Retail: at the till (stand / window sticker)
  - Trades and mobile services: at job sign-off (card / keyring / phone tag)
  - Car dealers: at handover (AutoTrader + Google card)

- **H2 Step 5: Reply to every review** (replies show you're listening and encourage more. Link to the review management trial.)
