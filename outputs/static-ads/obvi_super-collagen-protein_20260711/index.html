Research Notes

Sourced via web search on myobvi.com public product info (no page fetch available in this run): 19g protein per serving, hydrolyzed grass-fed bovine collagen peptides (types I & III), biotin, hyaluronic acid, keratin, vitamin C & E, calcium, iron, magnesium. Keto-friendly, soy-free, gluten-free, dairy-free, no added sugar, no GMOs. Reviewers commonly mention brighter-looking skin, stronger/shinier hair, and joints feeling better after about a month of use. Obvi runs an ongoing Subscribe & Save program. No verifiable current discount percentage, star rating, or review count was found this run, so those were deliberately left out rather than invented.

Product image note: no real product photo was available in this run, so a photoreal AI mockup (gpt-image, "low" render quality due to sandbox time limits — text overlays still rendered sharp and legible on inspection) was generated and used as the reference for all four ad edits, keeping the tub consistent across the set.
