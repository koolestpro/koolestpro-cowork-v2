## Finding 2 — The save rate collapse is the content drift, measured

**Saves are the buying-intent metric for a service provider.** A view is attention. A save is "I will need this later," which for a store owner means "this person can solve something for me."

| Year | Saves per 1,000 views |
|---|---|
| 2022 | **8.3** |
| 2023 | 6.3 |
| 2024 | 2.4 |
| 2025 | 0.9 |

Save rate fell by roughly 9x while view counts held steady. The content kept getting attention and stopped generating intent. This is the same drift visible on LinkedIn, and here it is quantified.

### The rule this produces

**Posts that hand a store owner a usable thing get saved. Posts about Ridwan, about design craft, or about abstract principles get nothing.**

## Finding 5 — 491 followers produced three clients

Reach is not the lever. The account never crossed 500 followers and still converted three paying clients, one of whom found him and came to his DMs unprompted.

This matters for planning: the goal is not to grow the account. It is to publish content that makes the right small number of store owners save it and message him.

---

## What this means for the rebuild

**Two formats, both proven, both automatable:**

1. **Transformation showcase** — the highest-reach format that also holds a strong save rate. Design or old page becomes finished, functional store. 12-20 seconds, no voiceover, music only. This is the format the growth plan's F1a engine generates automatically from the weekly CRO Fix artifacts.

2. **Utility post** — the highest save rates in the entire account (14-25 per 1,000). A tool, a free resource, a specific tactic a store owner can use today. Lower reach, highest intent per view. Cheap to produce: a screen recording of the tool plus text overlays.

**Rules the data supports:**

- Keep it under 20 seconds where possible
- Never make the video about Ridwan. The intro-style post is the clearest failure in the dataset
- Never aim at designers. The Illustrator tutorial earned zero saves
- Attach a usable artifact to every post — a tool, a resource, a before/after, a tactic
- Abstract or motivational statements without an artifact earn nothing
- **Track save rate, not views.** Views measure attention. Saves measure intent

**Cadence:** 35 posts in 2022 built the base that 2023's winners landed on. Volume precedes payoff by roughly a year. A weekly automated video is a realistic and sufficient restart.

**Fix the bio.** It currently points at email. Once the domain exists it should point there.
