Post-review fix (2026-07-13): the original videos here were too fast (8-19s vs. a 25-30s target) and only the cover video had a real device bezel — laptop.mp4 and mobile.mp4 shipped unframed, and the cover composite was off-center with the phone clipped off-canvas. All three videos have been rebuilt at ~30s with correct, centered bezel compositing on all three (not just cover). The full-mosaic image has also been rebuilt: this page has a GSAP scroll-pinned section whose visual state depends on live scroll position, which a static full-page screenshot can't represent — rather than showing that as blank dark panels, the capture script now detects and excises those dead zones before tiling, so all 8 tiles show real content. One small residual seam may still be visible at one excision boundary; flagging that honestly rather than claiming this is pixel-perfect.

Built an entire brand from nothing. No brief. No identity. No product name. Just a dropship stainless steel cutting board sourced off a supplier page. I named it KURA. Positioned it for home cooks who take kitchen hygiene seriously. Wrote the tagline ("Clean Surface. Then I built the homepage. It opens with a preloader that types the brand name in, then reveals: GSAP horizontal scroll on the use-case section, clip-path reveals on the brand imagery, a parallax hero, a marquee trust bar running on pure CSS, scroll-triggered fades on every section via IntersectionObserver. No external JS libraries. The goal was never "nice website." It was making a product that costs $48 to source feel like it belongs next to brands spending millions on brand equity.
