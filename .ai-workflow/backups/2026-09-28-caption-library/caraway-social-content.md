### SA-CARAWAY-COMBO

`outputs/static-ads/caraway_fry-pan_20260909/combined-showcase.png` · linkedin, instagram

```
Four static ad directions for the same frying pan.

Caraway sells a $125 ceramic fry pan on one idea: no PTFE, no PFOA, no PFAS. That single fact can be sold four different ways, and which one you lead with changes who stops scrolling.

Problem first. Name the thing people are quietly worried about, then show the pan.
Benefit first. Hero shot, feature callouts, oven safe to 550, works on induction, wipes clean.
Proof first. 4.7 stars from 18,878 reviews and two real lines from verified buyers.
Offer first. Free shipping over $90, free returns, one button.

Same product, same price, same photo brief. Only the opening move changes.

Most brands pick one of these and run it into the ground. The cheap win is running all four at once and letting the cost per click tell you which problem your buyer actually has.

Concept work, not a Caraway commission. Every claim comes off their live product page.

#CRO #DTC #EcommerceMarketing #AdCreative
```
