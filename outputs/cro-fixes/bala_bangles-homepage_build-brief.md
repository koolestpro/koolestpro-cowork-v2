# Bala - Homepage first screen rebuild brief

Builder input (Lovable / Figma Make). This run used the fallback: Claude authored the page
directly from this brief (`.ai-workflow/build_bala_rebuild.py`). Keep any builder project
PRIVATE; never deploy a real brand's rebuild publicly.

Source of truth: https://shopbala.com/ and https://shopbala.com/products/bala-bangles (checked 2026-10-11)

Audience: US shoppers landing on the homepage from social, press or a friend, on a phone, who have heard "Bala" but don't yet know it's the wrist and ankle weights brand.
The one job of this page's first screen: **say what Bala sells, prove it, price it and give one way in.**

## Non-negotiable rules

- Real facts only. No invented numbers, quotes or claims (no free-shipping claim: shipping is calculated at checkout).
- Mobile first, 390px. Homepage, so the outcome headline is the H1.
- Real logo (header SVG wordmark) and real product photos, as-is. Never recreate the wordmark.
- No gradient text. One primary CTA, bundles as a secondary text link only.

## Image decision (revision 3)

- Research first: `references-for-posts/homepage-hero-research-2026-10-11/`. Four directions
  built on real assets (range flatlay, lifestyle top, full-bleed scrim, text first).
- Chosen: Bala's own `The-Studio-to-Street-Kit-BLUSH` flatlay. It shows weights and
  activewear together in one colorway, so the hero shows the range instead of one product.
  Contain-fit on a matching #FBEBEE field, product labels on the image.
- Rejected: the Pucci lifestyle shot (sold-out collab), the wrist close-up (narrows to Bangles).
- No AI generation needed.

## Sections (revision 3: homepage, centered, short)

1. Static announcement bar: save 10% when you bundle, HSA/FSA eligible
2. Header: menu, real logo, cart
3. Hero image: the kit flatlay with 4 product labels
4. Centered stack: 4.9 from 1,865 Bala Bangles reviews / H1 "Weights & activewear, but make it cute." / one-line sub / ONE CTA "Shop best sellers" / category pills / As seen in WSJ, goop, Glamour
5. Below: best-sellers rail with real prices, reasons strip, photos, verified reviews, bundle card, footer

## Do not

- a mood line as the only headline
- a hero that narrows a multi-category brand to one product
- a long left-aligned text block on the mobile hero
- a hairline-weight headline on video
- an outline-only main button
- the hero product and its rating thousands of pixels down
- a variant picker / single-product buy box on a homepage (it reads as a PDP)
- inventing ratings, counts, quotes or shipping offers; implying Bala is a client
