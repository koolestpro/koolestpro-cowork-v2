KURA Board - Advertorial Showcase

Advertorial for KURA, a concept kitchen brand with one product: a food grade 304 stainless steel cutting board. It is dressed as an article from a made-up magazine, The Kitchen Standard: masthead with an ADVERTORIAL label, a copper promo bar, a hygiene headline, a byline and a kitchen photo. Then a stats strip, three sections on the problem (knife grooves, bacteria, why plastic is no better), why steel, an expert quote, how the board works, one reader story, the differences in tiles, the offer with the price, and reviews. On desktop a small offer card rides in the right rail. All assets rendered live from replo-rebuilds/brands/kura/pages/kura-ad-01.
