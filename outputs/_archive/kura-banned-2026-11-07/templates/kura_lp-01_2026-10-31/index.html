KURA Board - Landing Page Showcase

Landing page for KURA, a concept kitchen brand with one product: a food grade stainless steel cutting board. No nav menu. The hero carries the claim, a kitchen photo and the button. Below it: the problem with wooden and plastic boards, the board on its own, six benefit tiles, reviews, three steps, the objections (knives, food safety, dishwasher), a 30 day guarantee, the offer with the price, and the FAQ. All assets rendered live from replo-rebuilds/brands/kura/pages/kura-lp-01.
