## Reels / TikTok: the action plan to build later

What exists: `outputs/tiktok_mockup/` pipeline (Playwright scroll capture, overlays, Groq TTS, iPhone bezel), 5 videos across content types, TikTok inbox drafts live (sandbox creds). Instagram Reels route exists in Make but **IG is paused** (see `docs/system/instagram-warning-investigation-2026-09-18.md`).

Plan, in order:
1. **Swipe file first.** A folder `references-for-posts/swipe-file/` with one sub-folder per saved post plus a one-line `why.md` ("why I saved this: hook / format / visual / editing"). Save posts with Instagram's own Save button and add them by **screenshot or screen recording**, not downloader sites (downloaders are the prime suspect for the IG warning).
2. **Format library from the swipe file.** Group saved posts into repeatable formats (e.g. "talking head + screen overlay", "store scroll roast", "before/after flip", "listicle reel", "POV freelancer"). Each format gets a one-page recipe: hook pattern, length, shot list, text-on-screen style, audio.
3. **Pick 3 video formats to start**, at least one that needs Ridwan on camera (faces grow audiences faster than screen recordings) and two that the pipeline can produce automatically.
4. **Weekly video cadence** that mixes types (per the standing rule that TikTok/Reels must span content TYPES, not edit styles): e.g. 1 roast/teardown, 1 build-in-public, 1 quick tip, 1 trend.
5. **Hooks bank.** First 1-2 seconds decide reach. Keep a running bank of hooks that worked (from swipe file + our own analytics).
6. **Measure** saves, shares, follows per post, not likes. Double down on the formats that bring follows.
