# The agency partner offer (white-label Shopify development)

Written 2026-10-01. Ridwan has never done white-label work, has no agency rate card, and wants a clear offer that is a no-brainer for an agency. This is the design. The numbers are a proposal for Ridwan to confirm; the market benchmarks come from a web search on 2026-10-01 and are not verified.

## Why an agency says yes

An agency that sells branding, design or paid ads wins Shopify build work it cannot deliver, or delivers late, or has to hire for. Three things matter to it: **margin** (it charges its client its own price and pays a wholesale rate), **capacity without hiring** (no salary, no idle months), and **low risk** (the first job must not hurt if it goes wrong). Search results on white-label pricing say agencies commonly resell at 2 to 3 times the partner cost for 50 to 70 percent gross margin, and that flat partner discounts of about 20 percent or tiered volume discounts of 10 to 20 percent are normal ([bsscommerce](https://bsscommerce.com/services/blog/white-label-development-pricing), [webhelpagency](https://webhelpagency.com/blog/white-label-woocommerce-development/)). Ridwan's retail ladder is already far below agency retail, so a further partner discount makes the margin very large.

## The offer: Founding Partner programme

1. **Wholesale partner rate card**, fixed price per project, shared on request (not in the first email).
2. **Founding partner terms** for the first few agencies: partner rates **locked for 12 months**, and the **first small task is a paid pilot at half the partner rate**. Risk to the agency is close to zero.
3. **They keep the client.** Work ships under the agency's brand. Ridwan is never named to the end client and never contacts the end client.
4. **Scope and turnaround confirmed per project before any work starts.** No dates promised in advance (project rule). An NDA on request.
5. **Fixed price, 50 percent up front, balance on delivery.** No hourly surprises.
6. **Honest about being new.** The email says the partner programme is new. It does not claim white-label history.

## Options considered

| Model | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Wholesale rate card (chosen)** | Simple, auditable, easy to say yes to |
| Revenue share (a percentage of what the agency charges its client) | Needs the agency to disclose its prices, hard to audit, slows the decision. Offer later only if an agency asks for it |
| Hourly rate | Agencies want fixed, resellable scope |
| Reserved capacity block (for example 10 hours a month) | Good second step once one pilot has gone well |

## How to be credible with no white-label history

1. **Be open that the programme is new**, and let the pilot price remove the risk.
2. **Lead with real direct client work** (Tap and Rate, Champion Biotics, Mother of Macros, Amina Zaid (name only, site down), scentsavenue.ng). Good development is what an agency buys; it never needs to be called white-label.
3. **Offer a free short dev review** of one of the agency's own live client sites, the same shape as the store audit. It shows quality and starts the conversation.
4. **A partner starter pack** (to build): a one-page capability sheet, the rate card, a simple intake brief form, an NDA template, and a plain description of how a project runs.
5. **Reply and quote fast.** Speed is the main trust signal for a new partner.
6. **After the first pilot**, ask permission for a testimonial and a case study that does not name the end client.
