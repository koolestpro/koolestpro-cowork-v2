LinkedIn Caption — SA-MATERIALKITCHEN-COMBO

Material Kitchen's Trio of Knives sits at a 5.0 rating from 472 reviews. I wanted to see how many honest angles I could pull out of that one product page.

Four ad directions, same three knives, nothing invented.

Problem: most people own a bulky 12-piece knife set and only ever reach for two blades in it. The Trio replaces the whole block with three full-tang, Japanese-steel knives.

Benefit: full-tang balance, a 13-degree edge, cryogenic tempering, and that 472-review rating. All of it is on their own product page, I didn't add anything.

Social proof: I could have written a testimonial that sounded better. Instead I used a real one, from a verified buyer named Mara T.: "Sleek design, perfect weight, and very sharp. I love them."

Offer: the set is actually $30 off right now, $225 down to $195, so the urgency ad leans on a real discount instead of a countdown timer that resets every week.

Four ads, one product, four different reasons to buy it depending on what the person scrolling past already believes.

The job here was picking which true fact to lead with for each kind of shopper.

This is concept work. I have no relationship with Material Kitchen, I just liked the knives enough to think through how I'd sell them.
