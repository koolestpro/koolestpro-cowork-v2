## Winner
- **Primary keyword:** how to get more google reviews
- **Supporting:** how to get google reviews, get google reviews, google review link, qr code for google reviews, google review card
- **Intent:** informational, with strong commercial undertone (CPC £7.38 on the head term)
- **Stage:** Awareness → Consideration · **Cluster:** C2 · **Money pages:** `/collections/special-offers`, `/collections/nfc-stands`, `/collections/nfc-cards`, `/pages/google-review-calculator`
- **Format:** practical guide by business moment, about 1,600 to 2,000 words
- **Angle:** "Most customers who'd happily review you just never get round to it. Here's how to make it a two-second job at the right moment, without breaking Google's rules."

**Why it converts at low head volume:** the reader is a business owner already trying to fix this exact problem, and the article's answer *is* the product (a tap point at the moment of happiness). It also feeds the free calculator and the review-management trial.
**Why we can beat the SERP:** the top results are generic US "16 to 20 ways" listicles (WordStream, AskNicely, TextMagic) plus Reddit. Only one UK result (loudlocal, DA 21, #9). None of them explains Google's in-person and incentive rules properly, and none is organised by *where* the customer is when they're happy.

**Cannibalisation check:** existing post `how-to-use-a-google-card-to-collect-more-google-reviews-in-store` is narrower (one product). The new post becomes the hub and links down to it. **Next cluster step:** refresh that post so it targets "google review card" (candidate 2).
