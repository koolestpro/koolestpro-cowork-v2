**Long-form LinkedIn caption (use with any of the six assets above)**
```
Most product pages ask you to believe something. A landing page has to argue for it.

This is HALOS, a red light therapy panel. Concept brand, my own build, no client attached.

The page sells one product at one price, and it only gets one shot.

Hero puts the claim and the offer on the same screen. Then the objection everyone already has: you tried the serums, they sit on the surface. Then the mechanism, 660nm and 850nm, before any benefit talk. Six benefit cards. Three testimonials, each attributed to a name and a city, because an anonymous quote persuades nobody. A three step ritual, so daily use reads as small instead of another chore. An FAQ that says the cheap LED mask objection out loud instead of hoping nobody thinks it. A 60 day guarantee.

Price comes last. By then the reader has a reason to care what the number is.

That ordering is most of the work. Move the price block up into the hero and the same page stops converting.

Built and scrollable, desktop and mobile. Not a Figma mockup.

If you sell one product and your page reads like a catalogue entry, this is the shape it should be.

Need a store like this built for your brand? Send me a message.

#ecommerce #shopify #cro #landingpage #webdesign
```
