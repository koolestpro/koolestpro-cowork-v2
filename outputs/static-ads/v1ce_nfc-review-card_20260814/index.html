Research Notes

Sourced via web search on v1ce.co and third-party review listings (Amazon UK, Trustpilot, Loox): the card is an NFC tap-to-review tool with a QR code as a fallback for phones without NFC, points to Google, Facebook, Trustpilot, or TripAdvisor, and the destination can be changed anytime without reprinting the card. Works on Android and iOS with no app download. Credit-card sized (85mm x 54mm), PVC or bamboo. Orders ship within 48 hours from UK, US, or UAE workshops. V1CE holds a 4.8 star rating from 2,375+ reviews across their product range (Loox), and pricing for the review card starts around $38 with free worldwide shipping on orders over $70. No brand-specific review count for this exact SKU was found, so the aggregate brand rating was used and framed as such rather than invented as product-specific.

Product image note: no real product photo was supplied this run, so a photoreal AI mockup of the card (gpt-image, "low" render quality) was generated first and used as the reference for all four ad edits, keeping the card consistent across the set. One ad (offer/urgency) needed a regeneration after the first pass mangled the dollar figures in the offer banner — the corrected version is what's shown here.
