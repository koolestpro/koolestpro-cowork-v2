**Content audience note (corrected 2026-08-02, twice — read this before assuming content doesn't work):**

Content is Ridwan's **best** client channel by a wide margin. Six clients trace to it: Jellys Glamour and two foreign PageFly clients from **TikTok**, plus aminazaid → justdarlings → scentsavenue from **LinkedIn/YouTube teaching content**. Fiverr produced two. Cold outreach has produced none because it has never been sent.

What stopped working is a change in *what the content asks for*:

- **Old content** — TikTok build videos ("watch me build a store") and YouTube/LinkedIn teaching ("here's how to fix yours"). These are **service signals**, and buyers responded.
- **Current content** — CRO craft carousels showing build quality. This is an **employment signal**, and the market responds accurately: recruiters and interviews, no projects.

The fix is not more content or better content. It is returning to content that asks to be hired for a job rather than for a role.
