01 — Core Identity
Who We Are

The foundational statements that define Koolestpro's purpose, direction, and difference.

Mission
We help Shopify store owners stop losing sales to bad design — by rebuilding pages that convert.
Vision
Every DTC brand deserves a store that works as hard as its products.
Purpose
Bridge the gap between beautiful design and measurable conversion — without sacrificing either.
Value Proposition
For DTC brands bleeding revenue through poor UX, Koolestpro is the CRO design studio that diagnoses what's killing your conversions — and rebuilds it so you can lift sales 15–30%.
Unlike generalist designers who just make things pretty, Koolestpro combines conversion strategy with Shopify-native execution — every design decision is tied to a measurable outcome.
Positioning Statement
Koolestpro is the CRO design studio for Shopify store owners who know their store looks fine but can't figure out why it doesn't convert — because good design and good conversion are the same thing.

03 — Messaging
What We Say

Ready-to-use pitches, audience-specific messages, and proof points for every channel.

Elevator Pitches
10 seconds — The Hook
"I help Shopify stores stop losing sales to bad design — by fixing the pages that should convert but don't."
30 seconds — The Problem + Proof
"Most Shopify stores look fine but convert terribly. That's because design and conversion strategy are treated as separate things — they're not. I audit stores for exactly where the revenue leaks are, then rebuild those pages with CRO baked into every decision. Clients typically see 15–30% lift on rebuilt pages."
60 seconds — The Full Pitch
"I'm a Shopify CRO and UX designer based in Nigeria. I work with DTC brands whose stores look professional but underperform on conversion — usually because the design and the strategy are two different people's jobs, so neither is done fully. I combine both. I start with a structured audit: I map the customer journey, identify every friction point, and quantify where revenue is leaking. Then I rebuild — not redesign — the pages that matter most. Every element has a reason: the placement of social proof, the hierarchy of the CTA, the scroll depth where the buyer makes their decision. I've worked on stores including Eddie Bauer and client brands in food, gadgets, and lifestyle DTC. The typical result is 15–30% lift in conversion on rebuilt pages."

06 — Brand Consistency
Pre-Publish Checklist

Run this before every piece of content goes live. Tap each item when confirmed.

Voice & Copy
0 / 6 checked
Opens with the problem or insight — not a warmup
Has a concrete, actionable takeaway
No filler phrases ("excited to share", "it's important to…")
Uses specific numbers or real client context where possible
CTA is clear and single — one ask, not three
Fits within one of the 3 content pillars
Visual & Design
0 / 6 checked
Colors are from the approved palette only
Gradient text uses SVG linearGradient (not CSS clip)
Fonts are Syne (headlines) + DM Mono (body) only
Real Ridwan photo used (not "R" placeholder)
Stat SVGs are 300px+ wide (no clipping on export)
.swb and UI chrome hidden in download export
Data & Integrity
0 / 5 checked
Post JSON has unique id field
folder path points to an actual existing HTML file
Status is Created / Ready (never auto-set Published)
Metrics cited are from real client work (no invented numbers)
SVG gradient IDs are unique (no collision across sections)
Channel Fit
0 / 5 checked
LinkedIn: carousel has 8–10 slides, hook in slide 1
Caption opens with the problem — not the solution
Fiverr: outcome-first, no jargon in first line
YouTube: step-by-step, screen-share, shows real work
Cold email: names specific problem spotted on their store
