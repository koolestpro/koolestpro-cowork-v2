- **Full-space, redundant labeling (2026-09-07).** No slide may end with a large empty/plain-background band below its content — this restates and sharpens the existing "no dead space" rule in TYPE SCALE — HARD FLOOR, with a specific failure mode found on a real run: a small 9-12px uppercase eyebrow/state tag (e.g. "Myth" / "Reality" when using the default contrarian-diagnostic format) is exactly the kind of element platform compression or a fast scroll can make invisible. Any slide whose whole point is a state/label like that must bake the label into something large and hard to miss (a 20px+ pill badge with the actual word) in addition to the small eyebrow tag, not instead of it — redundancy is the point. Also check every slide's page-counter/handle overlay for legibility against whatever is actually behind it at that position; give it a semi-opaque backdrop chip rather than assuming a dark background will always be there.

- **Verify flex layouts on the actual export, not the markup.** A `flex:1` container declared outside a real flex-column ancestor does nothing — this exact bug shipped once on a CRO Fix carousel (an evidence image meant to fill remaining vertical space just collapsed to its content height, leaving dead space below it). If a Tips slide layout relies on `flex:1` to fill remaining space, confirm on the exported PNG that it actually does.
