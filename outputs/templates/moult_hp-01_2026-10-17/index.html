MOULT Brush - Homepage Showcase

Homepage for MOULT, a concept pet grooming brand built around one product: a self-cleaning slicker brush. The hero sells the outcome (less fur on the sofa) before the product, then the brand story, the featured product card with price, one card each for dogs, cats and the clean-up, reviews, three brand promises, a last lifestyle CTA, the newsletter and the footer. All assets rendered live from replo-rebuilds/brands/moult/pages/moult-hp-01.

Built a homepage for MOULT, a pet grooming brand I made up. It's a concept build, not a client. The store sells one thing: a slicker brush that drops the fur in a single clump when you press a button. So the homepage has one job, getting a dog owner who is tired of lint rollers to that button. How I laid it out: ✅ the headline sells the clean sofa before it mentions the brush ✅ the story section names the annoying part, cleaning the brush itself ✅ the product card comes third, with the price and one button ✅ dogs, cats and the clean-up each get a card, then reviews Desktop and mobile run in the same order. I didn't drop a section for the small screen. #shopify #ecommerce #webdesign #homepage
