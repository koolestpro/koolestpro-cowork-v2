## SECTION B — EXPERT BRAND PRE-CALL QUESTIONS

For well-optimized brands with in-house teams. Shorter, more direct.

**Current state:**
- What's your conversion rate and where is it stuck?
- What's your test velocity — how many experiments per month?
- Walk me through your last 3 A/B tests. What did you learn from each?
- What has your team already ruled out? Why?
- What does your CRO team think is causing the problem?

**The specific engagement:**
- What area do you specifically want me to focus on?
- Is there a decision you're trying to make that this review should inform?
- Who reads the output — the CRO team, the exec team, or both?
- Any constraints I should know about (tech, budget, timeline)?

**Competitive landscape:**
- Who do you benchmark against?
- Who's doing something in your category you admire, even outside your direct competitors?
- Where do you think competitors are currently ahead of you?

**Success:**
- What would make this worth the investment?
- Is there a specific hypothesis you want validated or challenged?

## PRE-CALL DISCOVERY QUESTIONS
### @koolestpro — Client Audit Intake

Use these on your discovery call. You don't need to ask every question — read the room and pull what's relevant. The starred ones (★) are non-negotiable.

---

### THE BUSINESS

★ What do you sell and who is your ideal customer?

★ What's your average order value and your price point compared to competitors?

What's your biggest revenue driver right now — specific product, collection, or channel?

How long have you been running the store, and what does your current monthly revenue look like (ballpark)?

---

### TRAFFIC & ACQUISITION

★ Where does most of your traffic come from? (Meta ads, Google, TikTok, organic, email, influencer?)

★ What's your current conversion rate? Do you know your site-wide CR and your PDP CR separately?

Are you running paid ads? If so, what's your ROAS and what pages do the ads land on?

How does traffic behave — do you see strong traffic but weak conversion, or is traffic itself the problem?

---

### THE PROBLEM

★ What's the one thing you wish was performing better on your store?

Where do you think you're losing the most sales? (Checkout drop-off? Product page bounces? Cart abandonment?)

Have you made any recent changes to the store — redesign, new apps, new theme?

Are there any pages or sections you feel good about? (Helps me understand what not to touch.)

---

### WHAT THEY'VE TRIED

★ What have you already tried to improve conversion? What worked, what didn't?

Have you run any A/B tests? What did you learn?

Are you using any CRO tools — heatmaps, session recordings, exit surveys? (Hotjar, Lucky Orange, etc.)

---

### GOALS & EXPECTATIONS

★ What does success look like for you after this audit? What would you want to see change?

Is your priority quick wins or a longer-term strategy?

Do you have a developer or designer who will implement the recommendations, or are you looking for someone to handle that too?

What's your timeline — is there a sale, product launch, or seasonal push coming up I should know about?

---

### TECHNICAL CONTEXT

What Shopify theme are you running? Any major customizations?

Are there any apps on the store that affect the product page or checkout? (Reviews app, upsell app, subscription app?)

Any integrations I should be aware of — Klaviyo, Recharge, etc.?

---

### CLOSE THE CALL

Based on what you've told me, here's what I'll focus on: [summarise the 2-3 biggest areas you heard].

I'll get the audit to you by [date]. The deliverable includes [name the reports]. Any questions before we wrap up?
