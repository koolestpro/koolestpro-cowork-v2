③ Post Copy
Cold traffic doesn't bounce because your page is ugly. It's missing one of 5 parts.

You wrote a pre-sell page. It reads like an ad.

Cold traffic can smell an ad in one line. They leave before they ever see your product.

Here's the part most Shopify brands skip. A pre-sell page is not one big pitch. It's a sequence. Five parts, each doing one job.

The Hook, which earns permission to keep reading.

The Mirror, which says their frustration back to them.

The Discovery, which introduces your mechanism like it was found.

The Proof Layer, real numbers, not adjectives.

The Soft Bridge, which hands them to the product without pitching.

Skip one part and the whole thing collapses.

Straight to a PDP, cold traffic converts near 1 to 2%. Run it through this sequence first and that number moves.

This is the conversion layer between your ad and your product page.
