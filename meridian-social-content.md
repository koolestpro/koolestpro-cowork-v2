# Meridian — social content

Demo brand for **Template 01 (Watches and luxury accessories)**. MERIDIAN is a fictitious brand Ridwan invented to demonstrate the template. It is never a client, and no post here may imply otherwise.

## TS-MERIDIAN-COVER-IMG — LinkedIn

```
A watch seller does not have a traffic problem. He has a trust problem.

Someone is about to send you two hundred thousand Naira for something they have only seen in a carousel. Every doubt they have has to be answered before they will do that, and a WhatsApp thread is a bad place to answer them.

So I built what answering them properly looks like.

Meridian is a demo brand I made up. Eight watches, prices in Naira, pay on delivery in Lagos. Every reference has its own page with a zoomable gallery, a full specification table, the warranty terms written down, and the actual return window. Filter by type, sort by price, add to a bag, and the order still lands in WhatsApp so nothing about how you sell has to change.

The part that makes this repeatable is underneath. It is a template. One config file holds the brand, the colours, the fonts, the copy and the products, so the same engine dresses as a watch house or a skincare line in about half an hour.

Meridian is not a client. Nothing on it is for sale. It exists so a seller can look at the thing instead of imagining it.

Selling watches, jewellery or anything else people hesitate over? Yours can look like this. Send me a message.

#ecommerce #watches #luxuryretail #nigeriabusiness #webdesign
```
