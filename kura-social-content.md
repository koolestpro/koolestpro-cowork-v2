```
Built an entire brand from nothing.

No brief. No identity. No product name. Just a dropship stainless steel cutting board sourced off a supplier page.

The goal was never "nice website." It was making a product that costs $48 to source feel like it belongs next to brands spending millions on brand equity. That gap is design. It's copy. It's page speed. It's whether the above-the-fold gives someone a reason to keep reading.
```
