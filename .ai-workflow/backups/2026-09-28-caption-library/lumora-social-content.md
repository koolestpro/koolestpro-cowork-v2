# Lumora Skin — social content

Demo brand for **Template 02 (Skincare and beauty)**. Lumora is a fictitious brand Ridwan invented to demonstrate the template. It is never a client, and no post here may imply otherwise.

Built 2026-08-28. Machine-readable sibling: `lumora-social-content.json`. Update both together.

```
Most skincare brands I look at in Nigeria are selling out of their DMs.

Not by choice. The alternative was a website that costs more than a month of sales and still ends up looking like a template from 2016.

So I built what the alternative should look like.

Lumora Skin is a demo brand I made up. Twelve products, prices in Naira, pay on delivery in Lagos. You browse, you add to bag, and the order lands in WhatsApp already typed out. No card form, no account, no app to download.

Real skincare business, still taking orders in the DMs? Yours can look like this. Send me a message.
```

## Notes

The four X angles are deliberately different jobs, so they can go out across a week rather than as one thread. X1 shows the work. X2 makes the offer. X3 speaks to one vertical. X4 talks to builders and to the kind of buyer who likes seeing the machinery.

CTA framing follows the project rule for Template Showcase: every one closes toward "need a store built", never toward a CRO audit.
