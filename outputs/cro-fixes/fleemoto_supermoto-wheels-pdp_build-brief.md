# Build brief: FleeMoto supermoto wheel set PDP (2026-11-01)

Builder: Claude authored the page directly (fallback path in CLAUDE.md). This run is unattended, so no Lovable credits were spent; the brief below is what Lovable would get.

## Business first
- Sells: e-moto (electric dirt bike) parts. 8 products, $7.99 to $329.99. This set is $319.99, a pre-order.
- Buyers: owners of small e-motos (Yozma IN 10, Valintinsu EM5, Tuttio) who arrive from search or social and already know what supermoto wheels are. Their doubt is fitment and timing, not "what is this".
- Brand look: stock Horizon theme, black and white, Inter, text logo. Keep it: black, white, one chrome-grey accent. No invented colour story.

## References looked at (3+ live PDPs for the same product type)
1. Charged Cycle Works, ION 17/17 Supermoto Wheelset for Surron Ultra Bee: a year/make/model fitment search sits in the buy block, next to Add to cart; wheel sizes and what's included are spelled out; lead time for tyre-mounted sets is stated (1-2 weeks). We have never put fitment next to the button.
2. Charged Cycle Works, 17" Supermoto Wheelset (Light Bee / E Ride Pro / Ventus One): the fit list lives in the description below reviews, far from the price. Same mistake as FleeMoto, so it shows what not to copy.
3. sur-ron.co.nz, Light Bee X Super Moto Wheel Kit: the pre-order label and the delivery estimate show before checkout (a modal states the wait and asks the buyer to acknowledge it). Honest timing before payment.

## Choice
Hero idea: the wheel pair as a studio cutout on a dark plinth (the product, not the bike), then title, price, a "Fits these bikes" check list and the pre-order button all on screen one. Structure taken from the ION page (fitment in the buy block) and the sur-ron.co.nz pre-order honesty, built in FleeMoto's own black/white Inter look.

## Image decision
Their photo 4 (wheel pair on white) is good enough once cut out; no AI image. Photos 1 and 2 (on the bike) go to an "On the bike" section.
