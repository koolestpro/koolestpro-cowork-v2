## PHASE 1 — INTERNAL BRIEF

**Core tension:** Stores optimize their PDP when CVR looks flat. But the page isn't always broken — the traffic routing is. Cold, warm, and hot buyers land on the same page and have completely different needs. Averaging 0.5% cold CVR with 8% hot CVR gives you a 2–3% overall rate that looks like a page problem.

**SUCCESs hook score:**
- Simple: One page can't serve three different mental states ✓
- Unexpected: Your CVR "problem" might be a routing problem, not a page problem ✓
- Concrete: Cold = trust before product. Warm = comparison not conviction. Hot = path not pitch. ✓
- Credible: Traffic temperature is one of the highest-leverage CRO levers with specific stage behaviors ✓
- Emotional: "Your page converts at 2%. Half your traffic doesn't know who you are yet." ✓
- Stories: Arc — one PDP for all → routing fix → matched funnel ✓

**Hook chosen:** "3 types of buyers. 1 page. That's the problem."
**Framework:** Cold/Warm/Hot awareness ladder + page type matching
**Big Idea:** "Every page is a product. Every click is a choice."

### LinkedIn Long-form

3 types of buyers land on your Shopify store.

Cold traffic doesn't know your brand.
Warm traffic is comparing you to alternatives.
Hot traffic has mentally decided — they just need to click.

You're showing all three the same page.

When CVR looks flat, the instinct is to fix the page. But a page converting 8% of hot buyers and 0.5% of cold ones averages to around 2%. That number isn't telling you the page is broken. It's telling you the routing is wrong.

Here's what each stage actually needs:

→ Cold traffic: Proof before product. Social proof above the fold. Problem-first copy that names their situation before naming your solution. Brand context in two sentences.

→ Warm traffic: Comparison, not persuasion. They're in research mode — evaluating you against the obvious alternative. Give them a comparison table, benefit-led copy, and reviews that answer the objection they're holding.

→ Hot traffic: A path, not a pitch. Buy button above the fold. Genuine urgency if you have it. Nothing between their intent and the checkout.

The routing fix:
Cold traffic → pre-sell page or quiz funnel (not your PDP)
Warm retargeting → full social-proof PDP
Cart abandons → stripped offer page, one button

Your next CRO win probably isn't on the product page. It's in knowing which buyer you're actually talking to.
