12 Shopify page types. Most stores only use 3.

The ones you're missing are the ones converting at 25–40%.

Everyone builds the same 4 pages: homepage, PDP, collection, landing page.

That's the floor — not the strategy.

Here's what top brands build that most stores never get around to:

→ Advertorial — reads like editorial content, converts like a landing page. Run cold traffic here before showing them a price.

→ Listicle — "7 reasons X beats Y." Ranks on Google AND pre-sells simultaneously. One page doing two jobs.

→ Quiz / Funnel — 25–40% conversion rate. Personalised product recommendations. Nearly 10× the Shopify average.

→ Pre-Sell / Bridge — sits between your ad and PDP. Builds context, handles objections, hands off a warm buyer.

→ Comparison / VS Page — captures buyers already in decision mode, searching "[your product] vs [competitor]."

→ Bundle Page — dedicated page for product combos. Lifts AOV without touching your discount structure.

→ Gift Guide Page — curated by occasion or recipient. Captures seasonal intent from email, ads, and SEO.

→ Post-Purchase / Thank You Page — your buyer just said yes. Their wallet is open. Most stores show an order number. Smart stores show another offer.

Every page has one job. When you ask one page to do ten, it does none of them well.
