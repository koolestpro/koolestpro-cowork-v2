## 1. Store identity
- **Sells:** NFC + QR "tap" products (cards, stands, table coins, tiles, window stickers, keyrings, phone tags) that send a customer's phone straight to a business's review or social page. It also sells a managed **review management service** (replies to Google and Tripadvisor reviews, fake-review reporting, monthly reporting).
- **Problem solved:** happy customers rarely leave reviews because finding the review page takes effort. Tap and Rate turns that into a single tap at the counter or table.
- **Serves:** UK local, physical-location businesses. The site names restaurants, cafés, retail, tradesmen, healthcare, automotive, salons and spas, hotels, dentists, estate agents and gyms (`/pages/review-management-1`).
- **Category:** NFC review cards / review-generation hardware + reputation management.
- **Market:** UK (GBP, British English). The store also serves `/de-de/` and `/en-ie/` locale copies, which are probably auto-generated and are not a focus.

## 3. Customers
- **Hospitality** (restaurants, cafés, pubs, hotels): table coins, stands, Tripadvisor products. Pain: reviews only come from unhappy guests.
- **Hair, beauty, salons and barbers**: Booksy, Treatwell and Fresha collections, stands at reception. Pain: booking-platform reviews vs Google reviews.
- **Motor trade**: AutoTrader card. Pain: dealer ratings drive leads.
- **Trades and mobile services**: cards, keyrings, phone tags. Pain: there's no counter to put a stand on.
- **Retail**: window stickers, stands at the till.
- **Social-first businesses**: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp products for followers rather than reviews.
- **Objections (from the FAQ):** "Do I pay monthly?" "Does it need Wi-Fi or charging?" "Will it work on my customers' phones?" "How do I set it up?" Content should answer these before the buyer asks.

## 4. Brand and site
- **Voice:** friendly, direct, upbeat, small-business plain English, a bit salesy ("Say goodbye to ongoing fees!", "Sit back and relax while we take care of the technical side!"). Short sentences, exclamation marks, second person.
  - Use: "tap", "in seconds", "no app needed", "ready to use", "one-off cost", "grow your reviews".
  - Avoid in our content: corporate jargon, "leverage", "revolutionise", "digital journey", and anything that sounds like an agency. Tone down their exclamation habit in long-form articles to one or two per post at most.
- **Visual style:** dark/black and white product photography plus bamboo-effect variants. Platform brand colours appear on the platform products. Clean, product-on-plain-background shots, so real photos work for featured images.
- **Navigation:** Shop (Bundles, Stands, Cards, Stickers, Bulk orders, Custom, Coins, Tiles, Keyrings, Phone tags), Free Google Review Calculator, Review Management, Support (Affiliates and Partners, Bulk and Custom, FAQ, How it works, Contact).
- **Conversion points:** add to cart, bundles, custom/bulk quote form (`/pages/bulk-orders`, `/pages/contact-custom`), review-management trial form, WhatsApp/phone.

## 9. Site issues spotted (feed into quick wins / the client report)
1. **Placeholder testimonial text in the homepage HTML**: the reviews block contains "Rodell Cruz 3 days ago Duis at nu…" (lorem ipsum). Check whether it's visible on the rendered page or only sitting in a hidden template. Either way Google can read it. This is a trust and quality problem. Fix first.
2. **Inconsistent voucher** (£100 vs £200) and inconsistent stats across pages.
3. **Wrong-intent rankings** on gift-card and virtual-currency queries. The product titles "Snapchat Plus card", "TikTok coins" and "Fresha gift card" are pulling irrelevant traffic. Retitle them to make the use clear (e.g. "TikTok NFC Table Coin for Followers").
4. **Duplicate and option collections are indexable** (`nfc-cards` vs `cards`, `*-options`, "Smart Cards (All products Offline)"). These need noindex or consolidation.
5. **/de-de/ and /en-ie/ locale copies** rank for odd terms (e.g. `/de-de/products/fresha-card`). Check the hreflang setup and whether these markets are wanted.
6. **Thin blog** (6 posts) with no clusters and few product links.
7. Product `/products/instagram-card-copy` is a duplicated handle ("-copy") that ranks for "tik tok card". Rename it and redirect.
8. Zero-priced utility products (3) may be indexable. Check them.
