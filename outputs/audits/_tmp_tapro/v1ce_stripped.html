⑤ Post Copy
Ever land on a page, fight through two pop-ups, and then get asked to pick a style and a quantity before anyone's told you why the product matters?

That's what happens on this NFC review-card store. Cookie modal first. Dismiss it, an email quiz pops up asking which "card is actually you." Dismiss that, and the cookie modal comes back. Once you're finally through, you're picking Google vs TripAdvisor vs Facebook before you've read a single benefit.

Here's what I changed and why:

1. Removed the blocking popup sequence, so the page loads straight to the product
2. Moved the price and top benefit line above the style picker (both were real and correct, just sequenced too late)
3. Kept the full benefit list and bulk-pricing table together lower down, for a buyer who's ready to compare quantities
4. Repositioned the chat bubble off the sticky CTA zone
5. Swapped the hero image for a clean shot with no competing brand in frame
6. Added a return/guarantee line near the CTA
7. Paired the 5-star rating with a supporting trust badge, since it's currently backed by 2 reviews

Same product, same brand, same price. Just put the reasons to buy before the decisions to make.
