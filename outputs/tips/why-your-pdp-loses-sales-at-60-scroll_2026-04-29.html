03 — Post Copy
Your PDP loses sales at 60% scroll — not at the hero.

Most advice tells you to fix above the fold. New photography. Rewritten headline. Faster page speed.

But your buyers aren't leaving at the top.

They scroll past your beautiful hero image — and somewhere in the middle of the page, they hit a wall. The size guide is buried. The specs are unformatted. The guarantee is in the footer. They decide this isn't worth figuring out, and they leave.

Here's how to zone your PDP and fix it:

→ Zone 1 (0–40%): Hero zone — hook, product image, primary CTA. Stop the scroll.
→ Zone 2 (40–70%): Decision zone — size guide, specs, social proof, trust signals. This is where you close.
→ Zone 3 (70–100%): Reinforcement zone — reviews, cross-sells, secondary CTA.

Most Shopify PDPs fail in Zone 2.

Not because the product is bad. Because the decision elements are in the wrong place, in the wrong format, at the wrong scroll depth.

Restructure Zone 2 before you touch anything else.
