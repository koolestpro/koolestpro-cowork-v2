HALOS Panel - "Five Reasons" Listicle Showcase

Listicle page for the HALOS Panel, a concept home red light therapy panel. Five numbered reasons answer the buyer's doubts first (two wavelengths, ten-minute sessions, sixty evenly lit diodes, use beyond the face, no ongoing spend), with a mid-page CTA strip after reason two. Then a buyer quote, a comparison table against a typical LED mask, the $20 first-panel offer, an embedded buy box, reviews, the brand story, a final CTA and FAQ. All assets rendered live from replo-rebuilds/brands/halos/pages/halos-li-02.

Built a listicle page for HALOS, a red light therapy brand I made up. It's a concept build, not a client. The angle is "five reasons people keep the panel after the trial ends". Each reason answers a doubt a buyer has before paying for a panel: does it work, will I actually use it, is it well made, what else is it good for, what does it cost me over time. Only after that does the page start selling. Comparison table, offer, buy box, reviews, FAQ. Desktop and mobile run in the same order, and I didn't cut anything for the small screen. #ecommerce #shopify #webdesign #landingpage
