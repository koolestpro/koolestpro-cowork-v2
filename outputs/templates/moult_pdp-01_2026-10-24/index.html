MOULT Brush - Product Page Showcase

Product page for MOULT, a concept pet grooming brand with one product: a self-cleaning slicker brush. The buy box (title, price, sizes, add to cart, three quick answers) carries the first screen. Below it: why it works, the problem with ordinary brushes, the brush up close with six feature tiles, a before and after, three steps, reviews, a results block, a comparison table, the FAQ, three promises and the newsletter. A sticky add to cart bar follows the scroll. All assets rendered live from replo-rebuilds/brands/moult/pages/moult-pdp-01.

Built a product page for MOULT, a pet brush brand I made up. It's a concept build, not a client. The brush drops the fur in one clump when you press the button on top. So the page has one job: get someone with a shedding dog to believe that before they scroll past the price. How I laid it out: ✅ title, price, sizes and the add to cart all sit on the first screen ✅ the questions people ask first (will it hurt, how do I clean it, which size) are answered right under the button ✅ the problem comes before the features: your brush is just moving fur around ✅ then a before and after with the same dog, and the three steps: brush, press, bin it ✅ on mobile, an add to cart bar follows you down the page The comparison table and the FAQ sit near the end, for the people who still need convincing.
