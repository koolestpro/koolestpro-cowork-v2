Brand Voice
Write like this ✓
Plain Nigerian English: direct, practical, and confident
Lead with a real problem, then show the diagnosis or fix
Use your own frameworks (Design First, 3C, USER)
Call out bad practices by name
Keep social copy easy to scan; use one clear idea per line when it helps
Use specific numbers only when you can verify them
Show real work, decisions, and results with context
Run the Humanizer pass before publishing: keep the voice, remove generic patterns, and verify claims
Never write like this ✗
Generic openings that delay the point
Vague tips without proof or context
Posting just to post (no angle, no point)
Over-explaining what you do instead of showing it
Engagement bait or a CTA that does not fit the post
Fluffy motivational quotes with no substance
Corporate tone or overly formal language
Invented metrics, unnamed sources, or borrowed client stories

Inbound + Outbound Machine

Client Acquisition System

Publish useful work, then start conversations with people for whom that work is relevant.

Inbound (Content → DMs)

Your posts give people a reason to know your thinking before they contact you.

1
Publish useful content on the pillar topics at a sustainable pace
2
Use a CTA only when it is relevant to the post and useful to the reader
3
Engage in comments — especially on posts from store owners
4
Follow up when someone has shown a real interest in a relevant CRO or store topic
Outbound (Proactive Cold Outreach)

Find stores where you can offer one useful, specific observation.

1
Find a relevant prospect through their store, LinkedIn, Instagram, or public ads
2
Do a 2-minute audit: find 2–3 specific problems on their store
3
Send a personalized DM with 1 specific observation (not a pitch)
4
If no reply in 48h → 1 follow up. Then move on.
Cold DM Templates
Template A — The Observation DM (LinkedIn / Instagram)
Hi [Name] — I was looking at [context] and noticed [specific, factual observation] on your product page.

For example: [what is visible, where it appears, and why a shopper may hesitate].

If you are already testing this, ignore this note. If not, I can share the first change I would test.
Template B — The Audit Offer DM
Hi [Name] — I can record a short audit of [store name] if it would be useful.

I would review the homepage, product page, and checkout, then show the first issue I would test and why.

If that would help, send the store link.
Template C — The Content-to-DM (use after they engage with your post)
Thanks for reading the post. If you have a Shopify store, send me the [product page / homepage] you are working on.

I can share one thing I would check first.
