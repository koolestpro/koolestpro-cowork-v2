COLD
STAGE 01: COLD
They don't know you.
Lead with proof,
not product.
Price means nothing when trust is zero.
Social Proof First
Reviews or a brand logo bar before the hero image, not buried below the fold.
Problem-First Copy
Name their situation before naming your product. They need to feel recognised before they'll listen.
Short Brand Context
Who you are and who you're for, in two sentences max. Cold traffic needs context, not enthusiasm.
@koolestpro
Ridwan - Shopify CRO & UX Designer
›
WARM
STAGE 02: WARM
They're comparing.
Give them a reason
to stop.
Warm traffic isn't asking "should I buy this?" They're asking "should I buy THIS or that other one?"
Comparison Table
Side-by-side vs the alternative they're already considering. Your wins need to be obvious at a glance.
Benefit-Led Copy
Not what it does. What it does for them, specifically. "Saves 2 hours weekly" beats "time-saving feature."
Objection-Addressing Reviews
Surface reviews that answer the hesitation they're holding, not just your highest-rated ones.
@koolestpro
Ridwan - Shopify CRO & UX Designer
›
HOT
STAGE 03: HOT
They've decided.
Get out
of their way.
Hot traffic made the decision mentally. Everything between intent and checkout costs you the sale.
Buy Button Above the Fold
No scrolling to find it. They've decided. Let them act on it without hunting for the button.
Genuine Urgency Only
Real low stock, real offer end date. Fake urgency trains buyers to ignore all urgency signals.
One Clear Path
No upsells on the PDP. One product, one offer, one button. That's the entire job of this page.

3 types of buyers land on your Shopify store. Cold traffic doesn't know your brand. Warm traffic is comparing you to alternatives. Hot traffic has mentally decided. They just need to click. You're showing all three the same page. When CVR looks flat, the instinct is to fix the page. But a page converting 8% of hot buyers and 0.5% of cold ones averages to around 2%. That number isn't telling you the page is broken. It's telling you the routing is wrong. Here's what each stage actually needs: → Cold traffic: Proof before product. Social proof above the fold. Problem-first copy that names their situation before naming your solution. Brand context in two sentences. → Warm traffic: Comparison, not persuasion. They're in research mode, evaluating you against the obvious alternative. Give them a comparison table, benefit-led copy, and reviews that answer the objection they're holding. → Hot traffic: A path, not a pitch. Buy button above the fold. Genuine urgency if you have it. Nothing between their intent and the checkout. The routing fix: Cold traffic → pre-sell page or quiz funnel (not your PDP) Warm retargeting → full social-proof PDP Cart abandons → stripped offer page, one button Your next CRO win probably isn't on the product page.
